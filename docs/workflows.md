# End-to-End Workflows — Cryo Scientific ERP

```
Customer Enquiry (ENQ-YYYY-0001)
         │
         ▼
Customer Master & Tech Requirements Capture
         │
         ▼
Cost Estimation & Internal BOM Costing (EST-YYYY-0001)
         │
         ▼
Estimation Approval by Engineering Manager
         │
         ▼
Follow-up Interactions (Phone / WhatsApp / Email / Meeting)
         │
         ▼
Lead Status Progression (Won / Lost / On Hold)
         │  (If Won)
         ▼
Quotation Creation & Versioning (QUO-YYYY-0001 V1, V2...)
         │
         ▼
Quotation Approval & PDF Dispatch to Customer
         │
         ▼
Customer Negotiation & Revision Loop (V1 -> V2...)
         │
         ▼
Customer Formally Accepts Exact Quotation Version
         │
         ▼
Proforma Invoice Generation (PI-YYYY-0001)
         │
         ▼
Customer Purchase Order (PO) Received
         │
         ▼
3-Way Verification (Customer PO vs Accepted Quote vs Proforma Invoice)
         │
         ▼
Advance Payment Receipt & Accounts Verification (REC-YYYY-0001)
         │
         ▼
Sales Order Creation (SO-YYYY-0001)
         │
         ▼
Multi-Department Approval Chain:
  ├── Sales Approval
  ├── Accounts Approval
  ├── Technical Specification Approval
  ├── Production Feasibility Approval
  └── Management Final Approval
         │
         ▼
SO Confirmed & Officially Released to Production / Purchase
```
